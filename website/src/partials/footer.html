bkash-server & bkash-client

High-integrity, type-safe open-source TypeScript SDK for bKash Bangladesh payment integration. Designed for zero-downtime token rotation, strict financial verification, and multi-framework support.

Credential Security Guarantee

Customer wallet numbers, OTPs, and PINs are exclusively collected on the secure bKash-hosted portal. Our UI components, client SDK, and server adapters never see or collect your customer credentials.
